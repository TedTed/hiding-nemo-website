Trainings and advising

Focused expert help bringing confidence and clarity.

Upskill your teams and get expert advice

We can create and facilitate trainings on privacy-enhancing technologies, tailored to specific audiences. We can deliver crisp presentations that focus on strategy and business impact for executives, hands-on tutorials for engineers and data scientists, and everything in between.

We are also available for other kinds of speaking engagements: invited talks at events, media requests, etc. — don't hesitate to reach out with any inquiries.

We can facilitate trainings and deliver presentations in English or in French, in-person or online.

Relevant experience
  • Mentoring a team of Google engineers with no privacy background to become experts in anonymization technology, reviewing product launches and building differential privacy tooling.
  • Delivering trainings about differential privacy and Tumult Analytics to organizations such as the U.S. Census Bureau or the Wikimedia Foundation, allowing them to take ownership of privacy-critical data products and independently work on further iterations.
  • Giving dozens of presentations and interviews about privacy-enhancing technology to a wide range of audiences: scientists, privacy leaders, engineering teams, journalists, policymakers, and more. Some examples can be found on this page.
