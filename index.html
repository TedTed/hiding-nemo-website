About us

Dr. Damien Desfontaines founded Hiding Nemo to help organizations do more with data, while providing robust privacy protections to their users. He is a renowned expert in privacy-enhancing technology, with a focus on differential privacy. His career highlights include:

  • Leading the anonymization consulting team at Google, providing guidance on policy and technical design for dozens of high-profile launches.
  • Obtaining his PhD from ETH Zürich, publishing award-winning work on differential privacy in top-tier scientific venues.
  • Designing and building leading open-source software platforms for provably robust anonymization, like GoogleDP or Tumult Analytics.
  • Training dozens of people, in both technical and non-technical roles, to help them understand and deploy privacy-enhancing technologies for real-world use cases.

His blog post series on differential privacy is a reference introduction to provably robust anonymization methods, widely praised by technical and non-technical audiences alike.
