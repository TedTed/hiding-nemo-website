  • Defining the techncial privacy strategy for dozens of Google's uses of anonymization technology, translating high-level policy requirements into production-ready data pipelines.

During COVID, the world's health organizations all had a problem: they couldn't tell if the stay-at-home orders were working. Their only way of telling this would be to go to their offices, look out the windows, and count cars to see if there were fewer cars. Damien and his team were tasked with this and delivered, under extreme pressure, a solution which was lauded worldwide. Through his work, we safely shared data that impacted the decisions of leaders around the world.
